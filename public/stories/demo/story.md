## [item-01] The entrance hall

type: text

The print works was built in 1923, at the height of the town's chainmaking trade. Its entrance hall still carries the proportions of that confidence — high windows, a tiled floor worn smooth by a century of footsteps, and the faint smell of machine oil that never quite left the brick.

Stand here a moment before going in. This is where the working day began.
